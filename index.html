Thesis

Competitive gaming data lives in Discord threads and spreadsheets today. We extract it, structure it, and own the layer every future product builds on.

Dynasty Central proves willingness to pay: 81 subscribers at $1,184 MRR with ~95% extraction accuracy on real users. The pipeline behind it — AI-powered screenshot extraction — isn't game-specific. Every product we launch adds to a cross-title dataset that doesn't exist anywhere else. Consumer subscriptions fund the engine; over time, that dataset becomes licensable to teams, coaches, and platforms who need structured competitive gaming data they can't get anywhere else.

Principles

How we build.

Import beats entry

Every feature starts from the same question: can the machine read the screen so the player doesn't have to type it?

Persistence is the moat

Game saves get deleted. Consoles fail. We hold the data across seasons so history outlives any single save file.

Niche but pays

We don't chase the biggest player base. We chase the most invested one.
